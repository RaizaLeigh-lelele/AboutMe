FACT 01

CORALS ARE ANIMALS

coral reefs are built by tiny animals called coral polyps. a reef may look like a colorful underwater plant garden, but the builders are animals.

FACT 02

SEAGRASS IS NOT SEAWEED

seagrasses are flowering plants that adapted to living underwater. seaweed, meanwhile, belongs to a different group.

FACT 03

WHALE SHARKS ARE FISH

despite being big, whale sharks are fish, not whales. they are the largest known fish species.

FACT 04

OCTOPUSES HAVE THREE HEARTS

two hearts send blood toward the gills, while another pumps blood through the rest of the body.

FACT 05

MANGROVES ARE COASTAL POWERHOUSES

mangrove forests provide habitat, trap sediment, and can help protect coastlines from waves and storms.

FACT 06

TINY THINGS RUN THE OCEAN

phytoplankton are microscopic photosynthetic organisms that form the base of many marine food webs.

FACT 07

THE OCEAN CAN MAKE ITS OWN LIGHT

bioluminescent organisms produce light through chemical reactions. it is one reason the deep sea looks unreal.

FACT 08

THE PHILIPPINES IS IN THE CORAL TRIANGLE

the coral triangle is one of the world's major centers of marine biodiversity, with extremely rich coral reef ecosystems.
